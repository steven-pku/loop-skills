This is an English overview. The Chinese README and individual skill instructions contain the full product descriptions.

Loop Skills is a collection of independent, Chinese-first workflows for content, work, decisions, and academic preparation. Each skill clarifies the task, works from available evidence, reviews explicit criteria, and revises within a bounded budget.

![A bounded review cycle](assets/loop-cycle.svg)

Choose one skill rather than installing everything. The collection contains nine previously released products under `skills/` and six experimental research workflows under `labs/`. This source and distribution snapshot has not completed new host behavior validation. Experimental availability is not a stable release or a claim of verified behavior.

Provide source material and mark missing information as unknown. A score is a diagnostic aid, not proof of factual accuracy, successful outcomes, or permission to publish. Skills do not automatically call one another or operate external accounts.

Copy a complete product directory, retain its resources, and check loading in a new host session. Installation details and current limits are in the linked guide. Never overwrite a modified installation without preserving it first.

MIT; retain each product's attribution and third-party notices.

[Independent product downloads](https://github.com/steven-pku/loop-skills/releases/tag/source-2026.10.22) include a SHA-256 manifest and packaging revision. Product versions remain separate from the collection snapshot. See [migration and recovery](docs/migration.md).
