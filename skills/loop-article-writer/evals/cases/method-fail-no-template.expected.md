- Article type should be Practical Method (the brief says 教读者日更; the title is a how-to). Score on the Practical Method rubric.
- FATAL issue: "no practical template in a method article." The piece promises a method but gives only motivation (心态 / 热爱 / 坚持) — there is no copyable process, checklist, step sequence, or rule. A method article with no method blocks regardless of score.
- Practical Value should score very low (toward 0-6/25): the reader cannot copy any concrete process after reading.
- Material Density low: no real example, no case, all abstract encouragement.
- Should also flag generic motivational ending (`希望我的分享能给你一些启发` / `每天进步一点点`) as Ending Judgment failure + AI-tone-adjacent filler.
- May note empty concepts (相信坚持的力量 / 长期主义 / 找到你的热爱) as a second fatal-tier signal.
- Decision: Revise (fatal present). Revision direction = replace motivation with an actual repeatable mechanism (e.g. a fixed daily trigger, a minimum-output rule, a 选题库, a template per post type) before re-scoring; do not just polish wording.
