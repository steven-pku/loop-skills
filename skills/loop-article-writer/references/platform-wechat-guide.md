# Platform WeChat Guide

Use this reference when writing or polishing 公众号 articles.

This guide describes writing craft, not guaranteed platform distribution tactics. Any claim about current recommendation rules, folding behavior, or traffic weighting must be marked `〔待核·上线前实测〕`.

## Platform Job

公众号 is good for:

- opinion essays with a clear stance
- method articles that require explanation
- narrative case analysis
- newsletters or serialized thinking

It is weaker for:

- pure listicles without a point
- shallow platform-growth hacks
- posts that rely only on visual punch

## Distribution Reality (craft guidance, not platform rules)

Operator consensus holds that 公众号 distribution rewards how well an article holds attention — read-through, dwell time, and shares — rather than raw word count, with no direct length penalty; length matters only through its effect on completion 〔待核·上线前实测〕. Platform algorithms are not published and shift over time, so this guide gives craft direction, not numeric targets. (Operator-circulated figures, with sources, are kept in historical development notes, outside this package — they are unverified and deliberately kept out of these agent-facing instructions to avoid over-fitting to numbers.)

Craft implications:

- Earn the read line by line. Do not pad to hit a length, and do not cut a necessary case to stay short — write to be finished, not to be long.
- Open strong enough that the reader stays past the first screen.
- Longform is fine when it keeps moving; write to what the argument needs, not to a word count.

## Title

Good 公众号 titles usually make a promise or judgment:

- A concrete problem: `很多 AI 文章空，不是因为 AI 差`
- A stance: `长文写作者不要急着学短视频`
- A contrast: `你以为是流量问题，其实是论点问题`

Rules:

- Prefer one clear claim over clever wordplay.
- A question title must imply tension, not ask an empty question.
- If the title makes a hard factual claim, verify it or mark it.
- Do not write platform rule claims such as exact opening-rate tactics without `〔待核·上线前实测〕`.

## Opening

The opening should create recognition in the first few lines.

Good opening types:

1. Name a wrong common behavior.
2. Show a concrete scene.
3. State a counterintuitive judgment.
4. Quote a reader's real thought or objection.

Avoid:

```text
今天我们来聊聊……
本文将从三个方面展开……
随着时代的发展……
```

## Body Rhythm

公众号 can carry longer reasoning than 小红书, but it still needs movement.

Rules:

- Let each section answer one reader question.
- Use short paragraphs for turns and judgments.
- Use longer paragraphs only when explaining a case or mechanism.
- Add an objection before the article becomes too smooth.
- Prefer one developed case over three decorative examples.
- Do not overuse tables in a final article unless the reader needs comparison.

## Ending

The ending should leave a judgment, not a polite summary.

Good endings:

- a self-check
- a final stance
- a narrowed practical rule
- a next action that follows from the argument

Avoid:

```text
希望对你有所帮助。
欢迎点赞在看。
让我们共同期待。
```

## Publish Check

Before marking a 公众号 article publish-ready, check:

- Does the title carry a real promise or stance?
- Can the core stance be repeated in one sentence?
- Does the first screen create recognition?
- Is there at least one concrete case or observation?
- Are hard facts verified or marked?
- Does the ending make a judgment?
