# Platform Xiaohongshu Guide

Use this reference when writing or polishing 小红书 notes.

This guide describes writing craft, not guaranteed platform distribution tactics. Any claim about current recommendation rules, folding behavior, CTA penalties, or traffic weighting must be marked `〔待核·上线前实测〕`.

## Platform Job

小红书 is good for:

- concrete experience summaries
- strong first-person observations
- comparison, checklist, and save-worthy notes
- concise opinion notes with a visible reader benefit

It is weaker for:

- long abstract argument without visual rhythm
- encyclopedia explanations
- posts that need many hard facts but provide no sources

## Distribution Reality (craft guidance, not platform rules)

小红书 algorithms are not published and shift over time, so this guide gives craft direction, not numeric targets. (Operator-circulated figures — title length, the CES-style interaction weighting, tag counts — are kept with sources in historical development notes, outside this package; they are unverified / folklore and deliberately kept out of these agent-facing instructions.)

Craft direction:

- Keep titles short and front-load the strongest phrase — operator consensus holds the opening words carry the most display and search weight 〔待核·上线前实测〕.
- Saves, comments, shares, and follows are held to signal more than likes 〔待核·上线前实测〕. Write notes worth saving and worth replying to, not notes that fish for a like.
- Use a few precise topic tags, not a pile — operator consensus: more tags is not more reach 〔待核·上线前实测〕.

Two honesty notes:

- **图文 ≠ video traffic.** 小红书 increasingly favors mid-to-long video for raw reach 〔待核·上线前实测〕. Image-text notes (图文) are held to compete on search, saves, and long-tail discovery — not on a same-day reach race with video 〔待核·上线前实测〕. Write 图文 to be found and saved over time, not to win a reach spike.
- **Early keyword ≠ SEO stuffing.** Putting a real keyword in the first ~10 characters is held to make a note findable in search 〔待核·上线前实测〕. That is legibility, not keyword-density gaming. This skill still does not write generic SEO articles or play keyword-stuffing games — moat ④ (观点 ≠ SEO) holds.

## Title

小红书 titles need a faster hook than 公众号 titles.

Good title types:

- Pain: `AI 写出来很满，但没人想看`
- Result: `我把文章改顺的 5 个检查点`
- Contradiction: `你缺的不是提示词，是改稿规则`
- Identity: `写公众号的人，别照搬小红书节奏`

Rules:

- Put the strongest phrase early.
- Do not rely on vague labels like `干货`, `深度`, or `必看`.
- Exact current title folding behavior is time-sensitive; mark such claims `〔待核·上线前实测〕`.

## Opening

The first sentence should make the reader feel "this is about me."

Good opening types:

1. Direct reader scene.
2. A blunt diagnosis.
3. A before/after contrast.
4. A one-line result from experience.

Avoid:

```text
最近很多人都在关注……
今天给大家分享……
我将从以下几个方面……
```

## Body Rhythm

Rules:

- Keep paragraphs visually light.
- Use line breaks to separate steps, turns, and warnings.
- Every few paragraphs, add a concrete observation, example, contrast, or self-check.
- Use bullets when the reader is likely to save the note.
- Avoid stacking abstractions even if the logic is correct.
- Emoji or symbol rhythm may help as a platform reader habit, but do not make it a requirement or a rule for this repository's own docs.

## Ending

小红书 endings should be short and useful.

Good endings:

- one self-check
- one save-worthy rule
- one question that invites a real reply
- one CTA at most, when it fits the note

Avoid:

```text
喜欢的话点赞收藏关注三连。
以上就是今天的全部内容。
希望大家都能越来越好。
```

## Publish Check

Before marking a 小红书 note publish-ready, check:

- Is the title immediately legible?
- Does the first sentence name a reader problem or result?
- Is the body scannable?
- Is there one save-worthy template, checklist, or judgment?
- Are hard facts verified or marked?
- Is the ending short and non-advertising?
